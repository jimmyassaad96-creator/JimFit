Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Overview

JimFit is a training-management app used by gyms, trainers, and their clients to build workout programs, log training sessions, and track progress. This policy explains what personal information JimFit collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

JimFit is provided to you by your gym or trainer as part of the coaching service they offer. If you have questions about a specific gym's use of the app, you can also reach out to your trainer directly — but for anything about the app itself, contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.

Information we collect

To provide the app's features, JimFit collects and stores:

  • Account information — name, email address, and (optionally) phone/WhatsApp contact info, used to create and identify your account.
  • Training data — workout logs, exercises, sets, reps, weights, and any notes you or your trainer add, so progress can be tracked over time.
  • Program assignments — which training program you're following and which trainer/gym you're linked to.
  • Payment status — whether a membership or session package is marked as paid, and any amount recorded as due. JimFit does not process payments or store card/bank details — this is just a manually entered status field for the gym's own tracking.

We only collect what's needed to run the app's core features — there is no advertising data collection, and JimFit does not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

How your information is used

  • To let you and your trainer view training programs, log workouts, and track progress over time.
  • To let a gym manager or owner oversee trainers and clients within their own gym.
  • To connect a client to the correct trainer when joining via a trainer's invite code.
  • To improve the app itself — for example, understanding which features are used, without collecting data for advertising purposes.

Where your information is stored

JimFit stores data using Supabase, a hosted database provider. Access to your data is restricted by account-level permissions: clients can see their own information and their assigned trainer; trainers can see their own clients; gym owners can see their gym's trainers and clients. Data is not visible across unrelated gyms or accounts.

Your choices and rights

  • You can ask your trainer or gym owner to update or correct your information at any time.
  • You can request that your account and training history be deleted by contacting us at the email below, or by asking your gym owner to remove your account from the app.
  • If you stop using JimFit, your data remains stored unless you or your gym requests deletion — it is not automatically shared or reused elsewhere.

Children's privacy

JimFit is intended for use by gyms, trainers, and adult clients. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a gym enrolls a minor as a client, that minor's use of the app should be supervised by a parent, guardian, or the gym in accordance with applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the app changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
